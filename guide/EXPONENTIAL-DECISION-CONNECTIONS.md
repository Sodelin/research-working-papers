# Exponential expressions: what is decidable under each input contract

The [15 October theorem packet](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/ddfac38cddac5b2ae0d53e25f0a61b9ae80e328d/research/2026-10-15-codex-expdec-reviewed-decisions/EXPONENTIAL-EXPRESSION-THEOREM.md), attributed to Codex, separates four useful contracts. It does not settle unrestricted real-exponential decidability.

For finite sums/products generated by algebraic polynomials and exp(Q), with polynomial Q over effectively encoded real algebraic numbers and no nested exponentials, expand H=sum_i P_i exp(Q_i). Group equal entire exponent polynomials, removing zero grouped coefficients. If a group remains, choose a nonzero P_* and W=P_* product_(i<j)(Q_i-Q_j). With D=deg(P_*)+sum_(i<j)deg(Q_i-Q_j), a tensor grid with D+1 rational coordinates per variable in a nonempty open rational box contains a point with W nonzero. Lindemann–Weierstrass then makes H nonzero there, and refined certified exponential enclosures eventually exclude zero. Empty groups certify identity. Algebraic-point sign is likewise decidable. The finite candidate count (D+1)^d is not a precision or bit-complexity bound. Identity on an open box is different from a zero at a point or deciding root existence.

Arbitrary finite nesting and first-order real quantification use the Macintyre–Wilkie decision theorem conditionally on real Schanuel. Log, division and positive-base powers require the written graph/domain guards; term witnesses must remain inside their atoms and their quantifiers. This is reuse of a conditional theorem, not removal of its hypothesis. The later [Berarducci–Gallinaro preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.08365) also keeps conditional status. [Mantova–Masser's complex solvability results](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.05592) concern another existence problem; our real restricted identity procedure neither replaces nor extends them by itself.

For weak formulas on nonempty compact rational boxes, with certified uniform domain guards, the packet specializes established [Gao–Avigad–Clarke bounded delta decisions](https://arxiv.org/abs/1204.6671). FALSE certifies falsity of the original formula; TRUE_DELTA certifies the stated relaxed formula. Both outputs can be legal in the overlap. A supplied robustness margin can make exact truth decidable, but equality fitting has zero robustness even at an exact fit, so that margin is no general exact-YES certificate. Residual units and which constraints are weakened matter.

Changing the coefficient encoding changes the problem. A certified approximation program returning a rational value is not an explicit algebraic encoding with decidable equality. The packet gives the familiar halting-style obstruction: c_M=2^(-t) if M halts at time t>=1, otherwise zero, is uniformly approximable with certified error 2^(-n), yet exact testing of exp(c_M)-1=0 would decide nonhalting. This is a representation boundary, not a new undecidability theorem for explicit algebraic inputs or the separate source-recognition problem.

What someone can use: a precise admissible grammar, finite nonidentity witness, guarded conditional/exact versus relaxed fitting specification, and a warning against silently replacing algebraic inputs by arbitrary computable names. A practical implementation still needs its own representation, certificates, resources and integration. The source has scoped AI reviews and saved controls, not a general Lean proof, fresh solver run, human review or exhaustive novelty audit. Unrestricted root existence and efficient bit complexity remain outside the established restricted claim. Appropriate next work is expert attribution/statement comparison and a scoped implementation or formalization project, with funding and responsibilities agreed before future joint work.
