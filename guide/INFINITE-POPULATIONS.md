# Infinite populations: avoidance, host obstructions and maximal clusters

The retained mathematics gives precise candidate answers to several questions about infinite labelled populations. It classifies binary sequence avoidance in one capped model, rules out universal hosts under specified embeddings, characterizes matching-path failure by ranks, and proves existence of maximal clusters under a fixed founder window. These are exact infinite-graph statements, not methods for inferring a real biosphere from finite genetic observations.

This Codex/OpenAI assessment reads four selected guides, the exact capped-host AI review and two selected Lean source bodies. It preserves historical checking as historical evidence, without a new Lean run, whole-proof audit, priority decision or biological validation.

## Published questions and the actual answer categories

[Alexander's 2013 paper, Section 6](https://arxiv.org/html/1212.0186v2#S6) asks about universal populations avoiding a fixed word, ordinal characterizations of populations realizing a word, and whether any non-eventually-periodic words are unavoidable. These exact primary questions and definitions were inspected here. They do not choose every possible embedding category or prescribe a rank spectrum.

The retained results address distinct readings. A binary permanent-gender cap-two construction supplies avoidance witnesses for non-eventually-periodic words, conditional on the recorded proof and model translation. Host obstructions depend on allowed degrees, root budgets, terminal vertices and embeddings. Well-founded ranks give a classical characterization of failure, but not necessarily a new structural classification. The sources do not establish worldwide priority or a current exhaustive census of the questions' status.

A separate [2026 paper, Section 6](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.05274v1#S6), proves a maximal-cluster result using common ancestry and reflection, then seeks qualitatively different constraints, especially without common ancestry. The retained founder-window theorem supplies a concrete mathematical candidate; biological plausibility and the intended qualitative difference remain judgments outside its formal conclusion.

## Binary avoidance at the balanced child cap

In the retained permanent-gender model, an infinite naturally indexed chronological population has finite birth prefixes and finitely many roots; every nonroot has a parent of each Boolean gender and every vertex has at most d children. Paths can start anywhere and read source-vertex genders. The source states that a population avoiding a fixed infinite binary word exists exactly when d is at least two and that word is not eventually periodic.

The case d below two has no eligible infinite populations, so the corresponding universal realization assertion is vacuous. At cap two, eventually periodic unavoidability retains Alexander's prior contribution. The negative direction uses a target-dependent labelled construction and a classical directed line-graph conversion. Its three-root witness is not proved root-minimal. Its unlabelled geometry can be fixed across targets while its gender assignment depends on the target: it is not one fixed gendered population avoiding all aperiodic words.

The practical mathematical value is a sharp model-specific existence threshold and a concrete witness family. It supplies neither a finite search bound for an arbitrary target nor the full finite-alphabet manuscript's proof audit. A broader binary conclusion needs the declared translation from permanent vertex genders to edge labels.

## Two nonuniversality results with different quantifiers

The balanced-cap hand theorem fixes k≥2 required parent labels and child cap k. If the avoider class is nonempty, every eligible host U has a nonembeddable avoider Q. Injective adjacency embeddings may ignore directions, labels, dates and roots. Chronological cut width is bounded by the host's finite root count. A sufficiently large finite grid cannot fit that width; adding a padded finite grid disjointly to an existing avoider preserves its infinite path language.

The construction allows additional roots, disconnected finite components and terminal vertices. Its quantifiers are `for every U, there exists Q`; Q depends on U. The exact [AI review](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/27b143c73a4ac272b560b2974d83ff96df738531/research/2026-10-09-dot-alexander-capped-universality-2055z/INDEPENDENT-REVIEW.md)'s final-delta section accepts proof SHA-256 `69c5dbe197b464fae37ccf3bfb457e374371f4232e80e4a2d4440c186a6319a7`, including fixed-source-gender row-striping. Its opening hash names an earlier version. This is scoped hand acceptance, not a Lean certificate.

The terminal-clone result instead starts with one actual avoider and defeats a **countable family** of locally finite population hosts under injective maps with any finite global edge-stretch bound. It retains exact incoming parents after selection and makes extra copies terminal. It need not preserve a uniform child cap. Maps that are noninjective or have unbounded stretch are outside that statement.

Neither result can be substituted for the other. Connected-only, terminal-free, fixed-root-budget and path-subdivision embedding classes require their own arguments. Classical local-growth diagonal and width/interval techniques retain prior credit; a checked crossing-count supplier does not compile the later grid corollary by itself.

## Reachable phases, ranks and productive pruning

Matching states must be reachable pairs `(vertex,phase)` reached by an actual prefix of the target word. Arbitrary vertex/phase pairs can include unreachable suffixes and falsely suggest a whole-target realization. On the correct state space, avoidance is equivalent to well-founded continuation and a strictly decreasing ordinal certificate. The inspected `GenericOrdinalCertificate` source explicitly uses standard well-founded-rank machinery and claims no new hierarchy.

With finite matching branching, a mortal state has an attained finite maximum continuation length. Repeated deletion of states with no remaining successor identifies the live core at the intersection of finite rounds, and the mortal heights are least natural decreasing certificates. Without finite branching, a root with chains of every finite length can survive all finite rounds while having no infinite continuation. An artificial root above all possible starts is yet another object: it can have infinite branching and rank omega even when each fixed-start tree is finite. These ranks do not distinguish every avoider structurally or decide arbitrary infinite-graph inputs.

The complete finite-fibre blow-up preserves every copy's matching heights under its hypotheses. Terminal cloning preserves a selected infinite language but can give new terminal copies height zero. No retained theorem combines all those preservation properties into one construction.

Productive-core pruning in the actual chronological population preserves infinite word language and supplied child caps. A separate deletion-only repair on a **fixed infinite retained vertex set** exists precisely when only finitely many retained vertices lack some required parent label. Its canonical repair deletes incoming edges at those deficient vertices and minimizes deleted/root sets by inclusion. This preserves avoidance, but can destroy realizations or connectedness. It does not optimize over changed vertices or added edges.

The port adapter supplies a finite set of crossing-edge slots and an actual fair replacement schedule on a late tail. Finite slots do not imply an eventually periodic complete schedule. The periodic-language theorem additionally needs eventual periodicity of the meaningful slot/label schedule, rather than merely a finite state count. These are useful representation and applicability checks, not executable arbitrary-oracle recognizers.

## Maximal clusters under one fixed founder window

The founder theorem works on original natural-number organism IDs with real birthdates, strict chronological edges, finite strict birth sublevels and finite children. Equal birthdates are permitted and IDs need not follow chronological order. Fix the **same** nonnegative real duration for every candidate and competitor. Clusters must be connected, ancestry-convex, IAP and reflecting, with every founder born within that duration of the cluster's earliest birth.

The retained [final source](https://github.com/Sodelin/Work-on-Samuel-Alexander-Research-/blob/e2502c82ab9a77c00543932f775a71e5374221f7/real/RealSpeciesTheorem.lean) states every organism belongs to an inclusion-maximal cluster within that fixed class, using an actual seed and classical maximality. The internal birth-order reindexing is transported back to all original-graph competitors. A singleton is not a universal seed because it may fail reflection. An explicit two-founder graph with both founders at the earliest date shows that a zero-width window need not imply common ancestry; that example has its own written, not separately formalized, status.

The conclusion supplies existence without requiring common ancestry of the final cluster. It does not assert uniqueness, a partition, unrestricted maximality among every specieslike set, a runnable finder or biological adequacy. Its use of common-ancestor seeds and a Zorn mechanism also matters when assessing the published request for qualitatively different constraints. Historical 151-, 274- and 415-endpoint manifests are distinct; unchanged theorem sources do not make the manifests interchangeable.

## Finite certificates versus eventual guessing

The capped-host packet also gives an abstract observation connection. For exact response coordinates treated as **discrete**, a target profile has a finite determining set exactly when it is isolated among admitted profiles. A sound adaptive run stopping at that target has queried finitely many such coordinates. Existence of a finite certificate does not itself find it or decide arbitrary real equality.

An exact-comparison stream can support one-change eventual guessing while providing no finite safe acceptance time. This connects to the established distinction in [Alexander's guessability paper](https://arxiv.org/html/1401.1894), whose definition and introductory characterization were inspected here. The retained zero-stream/single-defect illustration is abstract: it is not a family of admitted G4 biological sources. Discrete exact-response topology is also not a substitute for noisy real measurement topology. No G3 witness bound or general G4 stopping theorem follows from the analogy.
