# G6: honest conclusions from uncertain observations

The retained G6 work supplies a mathematical criterion for when finite noisy experiments can certify a property, allowing an honest procedure to abstain. It also supplies finite-prefix probability bounds and source-cloud constructions that could support such a procedure. These results connect source identifiability to sequential statistics and robust testing. They do not yet constitute an implemented all-source inference engine or an admitted sequencing pipeline.

## The useful target is a property shared by every robust explanation

The source class consists of finite positive population networks with the stated rooted-LSA/cut-child and calendar conditions, arbitrary finite original size and a declared natural routing mechanism. A later source-specific extension removes outer-labelled planarity. It does not remove the other assumptions. One source and parameter bank must answer all rows of a finite experiment. The target records distinct displayed quartet topologies and displayed splits; it is not switching multiplicity or observed gene-tree support.

For a finite observation profile e and target label Z, let I_Z^e be the admitted source-law image. A robust explanation belongs to the closure of that image at the true law. Intersecting these compatible target sets over nested complete profiles defines the robust fibre. Approximating witnesses may change with the profile; robust membership need not supply one finite source realizing the entire limiting law.

The accepted historical hand characterization says that, under its effective observation/cloud and fresh-locus assumptions, a uniformly α-honest procedure can issue a correct finite answer with probability at least 1−α when the requested finite-answer property H is constant on the robust fibre. If conflicting H values remain, the corresponding necessity theorem bounds the probability of a correct finite terminal answer by α. Honesty concerns the probability of ever issuing a wrong terminal answer across admitted sources; abstention is allowed. The procedure need not recognize the last shrink of an ambiguous candidate set.

This identifies a concrete use: report a supported **property or candidate set**, rather than force a fully specified source label when arbitrarily close alternatives disagree. It does not promise universal exact-target termination. The rare-switch source family gives a source-admitted approximation obstruction to that promise; it is not an exact equal-law collision. The full-calendar necessity concerns computable finite-read experiments, not arbitrary exact-real oracles.

## What must exist before the criterion becomes software

A finite rational cloud must approximate each target's source-law closure in both Hausdorff directions. Coverage of actual sources alone is insufficient: spurious cloud points also need a certified distance to an admitted witness. A rational proxy can lie outside the physical source image. The source-preserving representative and joint feasible-cell contracts supply the two directions mathematically, including exact emptiness only under complete feasibility. A timeout or missing witness cannot exclude a target.

The calendar compiler must preserve one rate per physical edge, original chronology and ties, joint rows and one physical witness per feasible cell. Local rational density on a fixed chronology stratum does not imply rational density inside every nonlinear constraint set. The reviewed construction takes one algebraic witness and then a suitable local rational bank; it does not fit a separate bank to each response.

Finite statistics add another contract. Fresh loci are the independent replicates; multiple records from one locus are one joint outcome. Reuse across nested profiles can be covered by a simultaneous error budget, but this does not create independence between records. Adaptive row choice must use the next fresh draw of the chosen row stream. The conservative separation-based sample bound is not optimal design cost, and need not be uniform when separation approaches zero.

## Numerical approximation has a shared-history interface

The rational Poisson-prefix component gives a finite count law and certified tail error using rational recurrences. Its normalized retained prefix and its residual-lumped proxy are different laws. Matching formulas or historical code checks do not prove Python-to-Lean refinement.

Common-subprobability arguments bound TV error for the **whole joint history**, followed by one deterministic readout. The number of coordinates in that readout does not multiply the error; each interval occurrence and each separately executed program still needs its budget. Numerical upper means retain the same physical iteration kernel. Changed physical rates need the separate shared-rate comparison, including holding probabilities, rather than only a bound on merger choices.

The natural-past result retains one joint law of the endpoint and old binned ages, using the original once-drawn register. It is not the product of separately fitted marginals. When that exact past is common to both laws, suffix-only approximation contributes only the suffix error under the legal cut/bin/completion assumptions. This is useful modular error accounting. It neither computes the unknown past nor establishes that inferred genealogies are exact observations from the source model.

## The external open-problem connection is robust testing cost

For full finite-alphabet TV corruption balls, separation from the wrong-property **closed** image must be strictly greater than 2β to permit certification for every allowed corruption at the clean law. Equality allows overlap. Pairwise midpoint geometry does not construct a physical source. A nonclosed wrong image can have every member farther than 2β while its closure touches the obstruction boundary. Additive Huber contamination has a different neighborhood, so its overlap threshold cannot be copied unchanged.

Vallinayagam, Pensia and Jog's 2026 preprint studies contamination-dependent binary-testing sample cost. Its conclusion asks whether the ε^(3/2) scale is the true instability threshold for TV/Huber testing, and leaves unequal contamination levels unresolved. Its examples retain separated uncertainty sets while sample cost changes sharply. The retained unequal-radius TV overlap identity answers a feasibility question, **not** these sample-complexity questions. A useful next research question is whether a source-admitted family yields comparable instability, with effective observed-image distances and a calibrated error model. No such reduction or solution is established here. [Primary preprint, Sections 4 and 9](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.24741v1).

## Acceptance conditions for reuse

A consumer must identify the actual source/target, observation channel, joint outcome, replicate dependence, allowed corruption mechanism, effective numeric encoding, cloud coverage and reverse witness bound. It must return UNKNOWN or abstain when these premises or computation are unavailable. The [receiver register](G6-CERTIFICATION-CONTRACTS.json) records these boundaries.

The selected formal receipts support fixed-cell completed observations, specified TV consumers, count certificates and particular natural-history components. They do not formalize the entire source catalogue, two-sided cloud algorithm or full statistical characterization. Earlier failed versions, later repairs, authenticated context reuse and uncompiled closure drafts retain their exact statuses. Historical audit counts do not measure discoveries or replace the all-size argument.
