# RNA class inference: connections to two published extension directions

The retained RNA/E8 work supplies conditional bridges from weighted deductions to structure-class probabilities, numerical traceback error and strict top-k certificates. Those components are directly relevant to two directions in PRISM's published discussion: adaptive sampling to a confidence threshold and more direct selected-class partition-mass computation. They are building blocks and acceptance criteria, not evidence that either complete extension has been implemented or generally solved.

This Codex/OpenAI assessment reads four retained guides and the original source-level scheduling diagnostic. It preserves dot/OpenAI and other original contributor attribution, historical component receipts and independent-review gaps. ## The external directions and existing prior work

PRISM analyzes a scaffold-conditioned hierarchical density-2 pseudoknot ensemble, using stochastic traceback to estimate structural-class mass. Section 5 proposes confidence-driven adaptive sampling and selected-class partition computation. These are published directions, not precise theorem conjectures or a certification of their present worldwide open status. The retained components can support those aims only after their executable, carrier and statistical interfaces are proved. [Primary discussion, page 30:16](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/storage/00lipics/lipics-vol390-wabi2026/LIPIcs.WABI.2026.30/LIPIcs.WABI.2026.30.pdf).

Computing selected shape masses and controlling residual mass have important prior art: RapidShapes computes exact selected RNA-shape probabilities in a different nested-RNA setting. Weighted hypergraph inference also predates the retained interfaces; Ponty and Saule explicitly assume unambiguous decomposition when separating conformation/energy models from applications. The relevant question is therefore source-faithful composition for the declared crossing-class ensemble, not inventing generic dynamic programming or residual-mass certification. ## What the weighted interpreter establishes

The formal source layers have different premises. An observer frame preserves the provider, allowance and local-weight view. An indexed recurrence needs every provider child to precede its parent, including occurrences later skipped. The derivation-sum layer additionally assumes exact semiring operations and sums all ordered derivations, retaining duplicate provider occurrences and repeated child slots. Noncommutative multiplication is permitted at that layer. Floating-point accumulation and runtime aliasing are not verified by declaring a semiring-valued model.

A derivation sum is not automatically a structure sum: two positive traces for one structure and one for another can bias the induced law. The Gamma admission bridge requires positive-output coverage/injectivity, matching energy weights up to its common gauge, geometry, lossless rendering and classifier agreement. It then identifies the ideal class law on the whole declared raw carrier. These are substantive source-specific premises, not assertions that the executable satisfies them.

The scanner/partner/schema components constrain represented pairs and finite supplied root trees. A bound `child.j≤parent.j` allows equality and is not a strict global schedule or fuel bound. A root decoder is not an admission proof for every actual runtime trace. Pair-map equivalence alone also need not establish a chosen bracket-string classifier's agreement.

## A useful diagnosed obstruction has a narrow conclusion

The retained 21-nucleotide source diagnostic has a positive-local WMBP production reading a later BE child in the original span schedule. This refutes the broad all-raw-provider/all-cache-cell earlier-child premise for that fixture. It does not refute the conditional interpreter theorem.

The same record reports that this parent was outside the productive root-reachable graph; its 37 productive root states were topologically processed and the cached/reference root values agreed at the reported precision. No root-law error is demonstrated. The useful next source obligation is a justified productive root-reachable schedule and observer boundary, explaining why excluded states cannot affect the target. Re-running the owner or adopting a reorder is not part of this assessment.

Separate retained diagnostics concern a VPR padding-factor discrepancy and classifier sensitivity to bracket rendering. Their bounded source/intervention evidence does not prove complete ensemble equivalence or every positive output's classifier mismatch. Original and corrected revisions must remain distinct. None of these results justifies an unqualified statement that the current PRISM release is wrong.

## Two routes to a strict top-k set

The exact route computes every selected class's inverse-image weight sum and the full positive partition for the **same** carrier, classifier and weights. If residual mass outside the selected set is strictly smaller than every selected class mass, it certifies a strict top-k set. This sufficient test may fail for a true top-k set. Maximizing one structure or trace does not supply its class sum.

The interval route requires valid bounds for discovered classes and an upper cap for every undiscovered class. Each selected lower bound must exceed all omitted upper bounds and the unseen cap. Soundness at a data-selected success time requires a simultaneous all-time/all-class event, not separate fixed-time coverage. Neither route resolves ties across the selected boundary, orders the selected classes internally or guarantees discovery/termination. Failure is abstention, not a contrary ranking.

A uniform discrepancy bound between target and actual sampling class masses can widen the certificate intervals. It must itself be justified: a fixed PRNG seed or one-step marginal uniformity does not prove fresh conditional random input along a traceback. The older finite-grid theorem and the later root-positive Gamma endpoint have different positivity assumptions; zero-mass branches require a supported-history adapter. The guide-derived midpoint supremum/product bound remains independently unreviewed, not a compiled new endpoint or an actual RNG guarantee.

## Concrete acceptance path

For direct class-mass computation, identify the actual productive carrier and schedule, prove the derivation-to-structure/class map and weight preservation, then supply exact sums or certified enclosures. For adaptive sampling, additionally prove the conditional sampling law, numerical/RNG discrepancy, simultaneous uncertainty and unseen-class cap; analyze whether and when the gap permits stopping. A finite local branch-count control does not bound all traceback fuel.
