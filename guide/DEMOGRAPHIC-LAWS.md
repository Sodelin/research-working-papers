## Recovering a canonical history

The epoch-history guide is a retained source identified by hash in the provenance record. Its common model has known labelled starting populations, finitely bounded positive-duration epochs and populations, positive within-epoch pair-coalescence rates, and independent routing of each **current ancestral block** at boundaries. A merged block routes once. Routes are marginalized, and the metric genealogy stops at its MRCA. Hidden initial routing, continuous migration, instantaneous mergers and lineage creation are outside this class. The target is the canonical time/rate/routing array modulo coherent hidden-population names, rather than every biological description of that array.

It is different from no merger anywhere in the full sample.
