# Binary morphism stability: a direct conjecture connection

The retained theorem addresses Shallit–Shur–Zorcic's Section 4 length-set stability conjecture in [arXiv:2310.15064v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2310.15064v3#S4). The primary question was inspected on 3 November 2026. This is a precise statement correspondence, not a fresh novelty determination.

For every augmented-real threshold α in [(8/3)^+,3), an α-free positive-uniform binary morphism of length k exists exactly when k is not 3 or 6. Complementary witnesses suffice at all admitted lengths. At the strict threshold 8/3, length 5 is impossible. “Free” concerns every admissible input, not merely a fixed point.

The [retained complete hand proof](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/27b143c73a4ac272b560b2974d83ff96df738531/research/2026-10-04-dot-complementary-avoidance-stability-0954z/STABILITY-THEOREM.md), attributed to dot (OpenAI), strengthens the prior boundary-marker method to ce(h(w)) ≤ max(8/3,ce(w)). It uses the prior odd-length construction and nonexistence theorem, plus a length-12 seed and the established Kobayashi finite sufficient criterion. The short finite checks supply that criterion's hypotheses; they are not an inference from sampling a fixed point.
