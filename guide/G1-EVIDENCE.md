## Question and result

Can a complicated ancestral network be reduced while preserving the answers
to a specified class of questions? The selected result says that for its
particular rooted, labelled, planar, cut-child, galled source class, one
decorated core can be chosen for all permitted later contexts. For `n ≥ 4`
labels, its numbers of retained hybrids, vertices and edges satisfy
`h ≤ 2n − 2`, `V ≤ 6n − 5` and `E ≤ 8n − 8`; the source records sharpness
examples attaining all three bounds for each such `n`.

The preserved observations are the stated completed **unranked**
forest/register/root-history and source-context laws. Parallel original arc
occurrences and original source/register information matter. Hidden serial
words can remain arbitrarily long even when the core is bounded. The result
does not itself bound an unknown G3 witness, preserve arbitrary timed
measurements, identify an unknown network, or validate a DNA-analysis pipeline.

## Concise argument and its source

Full Pro's `decorated-core` unit summarizes the existing argument: decompose
the admissible graph at bridges, suppress the allowed nonroot two-port blobs
into their actual serial decorations, and retain the root and branching
blobs. Counting ports in the remaining bridge tree gives the hybrid bound;
binary degree identities give the vertex and edge bounds. A repeated
two-hybrid construction supplies sharpness. The physical preservation step
retains the original serial words and composes the equal cut-boundary kernels
with the same permitted exterior programme. This summary depends on those
named graph, kernel and sharpness suppliers; it is not an independent proof
audit or a proof of shortest possible length.
