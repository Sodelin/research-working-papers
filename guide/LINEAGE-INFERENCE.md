# Lineage inference: scores, hidden graphs and realized histories

The retained lineage work helps decide what an inference result can mean. It characterizes one score's information content, gives a full-law ambiguity example for a particular hidden branching model, and supplies complexity/algorithm statements for optimizing a polytomy refinement. These are distinct uses. None alone identifies a biological cell-transition graph from measured barcodes or expression.

This Codex/OpenAI assessment reads three retained guides, the exact parent-retaining branching witness and two scoped historical AI reviews. The original arguments are attributed to dot/OpenAI and their source contributors. It is source/contract synthesis, not new independent proof, human peer review, an empirical analysis or a formal build.

## What the potency score can recover

For the specified finite differentiation DAGs, vertices are distinct nonempty potency subsets of the observed type alphabet, with strict set-decreasing edges and prescribed singleton reachability. The sampling-only zero-resolution objective minimizes missing-potency cost over compatible typed-tree labels. Equality of the score on **all** finite typed test trees characterizes the potency-vertex family and reachability relation. It does not identify redundant direct edges: adding a shortcut already implied by a path preserves reachability and the score signature.

That observation is relevant to interpreting Carta's maps, but its theorem is for the stated sampling-only objective. Carta's published method also uses additional criteria to choose displayed direct edges, including cellular flow. Score equivalence is not a claim that its complete method ignores those criteria or that its empirical results are wrong. [Primary methods, choosing map edges](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12982130/).

A retained finite example also changes the optimal extra potency state after pruning some leaves while retaining the type alphabet. This diagnoses lack of projectivity of that estimator under that objective. It is not an asymptotic inconsistency theorem or a refutation of empirical subsampling robustness.

## When a full stochastic observation still cannot determine a hidden graph

The separately reviewed branching theorem retains hidden transitions, births, deaths, a known initial state and coarse terminal emissions. Its fixed three-state [parent-retaining witness](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/c2650a5d8afc0be75cc8141f9f30d0907e961425/research/2026-10-22-dot-developmental-lineage-score-interface/PARENT-RETAINING-BIRTH-SPECIALIZATION.md) has two valid rate systems with five versus six positive direct transition edges but the same law of the complete timed genealogy with coarse labels. The proof conjugates the **killed** edge generator and transforms the bilinear birth operation, root and emissions consistently. Conjugating only the transition generator would omit the state-dependent birth/death terms.

A common neutral-barcode or sampling channel applied to that same preserved history retains equality when it does not distinguish the unresolved hidden states. Equal observation laws imply that a graph estimator cannot be correct with probability greater than one half at both members of this pair, even after independent repetitions of the same experiment. This is an exact model-specific ambiguity, not a limited-sample failure.

The witness uses cross-type parent-retaining births and two hidden states with the same emitted class. It does not cover division restricted to Xi→2Xi, separately observed hidden types or equality from every labelled hidden start. The retained source explicitly leaves identification for narrower state-preserving models dependent on a realization/observation theorem and an admitted rival class. It also does not certify observation minimality or a contradiction of an outside hidden-equivalence theorem.

Vaughan and Stadler's multitype birth–death work is related source-class prior. The retained coarse-emission model is a proposed observation extension, not a claim that their existing genomic observations have those unresolved labels. [Primary article](https://academic.oup.com/mbe/article/42/6/msaf130/8155703).

## Parameters and actual migration paths are different targets

The retained CTMC comparison separates a generator-support graph, the actual jump multigraph along branches, and a graph formed only by different node-endpoint labels. A two-state path can leave a tissue and return before the next node. Its endpoints then omit the excursion even when an exact endpoint law identifies the transition rate. A deterministic summary collision also shows that a full untimed endpoint-count graph can lose the placement needed to compute its pruned counterpart.

These statements do not establish that two complete stochastic observation laws coincide in the CTMC comparison, nor that BEAM reports the wrong target. Its endpoint-history interpretation is attributed to the retained guide. The primary BEAM page metadata was accessible in this pass, but subsequent content searches hit a browser challenge and the DOI full-text route failed; no fresh complete primary-model recheck is claimed. The exact observed channel, conditional barcode assumptions, graph target and pruning convention must accompany any comparison.

## A separate useful algorithmic result: refining a supplied lineage tree

With a fixed supplied differentiation DAG, the retained optimization chooses a binary refinement of an input lineage tree and a compatible potency labeling. The [hand theorem](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/c2650a5d8afc0be75cc8141f9f30d0907e961425/research/2026-10-22-dot-developmental-lineage-score-interface/POLYTOMY-REFINEMENT-COMPLEXITY.md) states NP-completeness for a restricted marked-star construction with unbounded type alphabet, unbounded multifurcation degree, composite already-binary child clades and repeated types. It does not establish hardness for fixed alphabet, bounded degree or a star whose children are only single leaves.

Its source-specific reduction from classical min-sum set cover preserves the optimum through an additive offset and a caterpillar exchange over **all** refinements. That offset does not automatically transfer a multiplicative approximation ratio. This extends the computational question beyond fixed-tree labeling; it is not a new proof of every complexity claim in Carta.

The same source supplies a general explicit-DAG subset dynamic program, exponential in child degree but polynomial for bounded maximum degree under its stated arithmetic/encoding conditions. This is a candidate exact receiver for unresolved polytomies when inputs and objective match. No current implementation or runtime measurement is established here.

## What formal evidence and a practical receiver still need

The lineage formal proposal remains source-only and unelaborated. The later archive work recovered and hashed registered SAT-hardness captures, superseding earlier unavailability only at the byte level. It did not compose proof objects or establish kernel/axiom closure. An exact all-word SAT/X3C/MSSC codec-and-machine bridge, compatible toolchain/imports and actual endpoint acceptance remain separate obligations. Large source/module counts or a declaration named NPComplete do not discharge them.

A practical consumer should first choose its target: potency reachability, a direct hidden graph, a realized path, an endpoint summary or a refinement score. Then specify its actual lineage/barcode/expression channel, preparation, hidden rivals, sampling and statistical loss. The [receiver register](LINEAGE-INFERENCE-REGISTER.json) records those choices and unsupported transfers.

These results can guide model selection, interpretation and algorithm design. Remaining work includes source-faithful observation adapters, efficient implemented refinement, independently checked proofs and prior comparison, empirical admission and suitable publication rights.
