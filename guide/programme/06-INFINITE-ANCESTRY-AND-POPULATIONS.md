# Infinite ancestry, populations, feedback and interval graphs

Here ancestry means a **nonempty** directed parent path. For a candidate set $S$, IAP means each member has finitely many descendants **or** finitely many nondescendants within $S$. “Specieslike” and “inspecies” are the source's mathematical predicates, not biological classifications. Dates, parent counts, owner maps and embedding categories are part of each theorem.

## Alexander: universality and finite-future ranks

**CB-ALEXANDER-001 — no balanced-cap universal host.** Fix a nonempty class avoiding one infinite label word, with a parent of every one of $k\ge2$ labels at nonroots and at most $k$ children. No member is universal under injective adjacency embeddings. The actual chronological crossing-conservation lemma bounds a host's crossing width through its finite root count, hence bounds its associated path decomposition. A finite eligible grid component can have larger width while respecting the same balanced cap. Add it disjointly to an avoiding source. Its finite paths add no infinite word, so the full infinite language is unchanged, but it cannot embed in the host. The fixed-source-gender version uses the corresponding source-admitted grid adapter. The finite component is allowed to have roots and terminals. Fixed root budgets, connected-only/no-terminal classes, larger host caps and edge-to-path embeddings are outside this proof. This is hand-accepted, not Lean-verified. [Exact construction and review entry](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/7206e66a8de0b04725924e32df822eca530b1143/research/2026-10-09-dot-alexander-capped-universality-2055z/README.md).

**CB-ALEXANDER-002 — no countable host family, without a fixed child cap.** Begin with an actual avoiding population $P$. Select one incoming parent of each label; this only shrinks its language. Replace each vertex $v$ by a positive finite fibre, retaining canonical copy $(v,0)$; every copy of a child receives its incoming edges from the canonical parent copies. Noncanonical copies are terminal. Root fibres are singletons. Projection and the canonical section preserve the selected infinite language exactly, dates remain strict and finite sublevels/child sets remain finite. Exact incoming-parent counts are preserved.

Choose an infinite ray in the selected population and enumerate all triples (host $U_j$, proposed image $u$ of its start, finite stretch $L$). At the corresponding depth $d$, make that fibre larger than the finite undirected host ball $B(u,Ld)$. A stretch-$L$ injective map must put the whole fibre in that smaller ball, impossible. One multiplicity sequence handles all triples. This proves countable nonuniversality even for bounded-stretch edge-to-path maps and arbitrary host avoidance status. It does **not** preserve a uniform child cap, exclude unbounded-stretch maps or address uncountably many hosts. [Construction and checked endpoints](https://github.com/Sodelin/Work-on-Samuel-Alexander-Research-/blob/e2502c82ab9a77c00543932f775a71e5374221f7/research/open-questions/continuation-2026-09-29/RESULTS.md).

**CB-ALEXANDER-003 — exact continuation ranks.** For word $s$, use reachable states $(v,j)$ witnessed by a path spelling its first $j$ letters. A successor is an edge labelled $s_j$ to phase $j+1$. Avoidance is equivalent to well-foundedness of this relation: an infinite chain plus its reaching prefix realizes $s$. Well-founded recursion gives a strictly decreasing ordinal rank, and such a rank forbids an infinite chain. Unreachable phases must not be included.

With finite per-label branching, repeatedly delete states without surviving successors. A state surviving every finite round has a surviving child: otherwise finitely many child failure times have a maximum. Choice then constructs an infinite continuation. Thus the intersection is exactly the live core and greatest successor fixed point. Its complement is forward closed. Every mortal state has an attained finite maximum continuation length $h$, by finite branching/König; it survives exactly rounds $n\le h$ and disappears at $h+1$. Induction proves $h$ pointwise least among decreasing natural certificates. This is a statewise dichotomy even when other starts realize $s$. The separate complete finite-fibre blow-up preserves all reachable phases and continuation lengths; it is not terminal cloning and need not preserve exact parent counts.

**CB-ALEXANDER-004 — maximal clusters with a real founder window.** Assume an INFINITE carrier and real birthdates strictly increase on edges, strict real sublevels and child sets are finite, and IDs are naturals but need not be chronological. For nonempty $S$, its earliest birthdate $m(S)$ is attained; internal founders have no strict ancestor in $S$. Fix one duration $\Delta$. Require connectivity, ancestry convexity, IAP, reflection and every founder's date $\le m(S)+\Delta$.

The ambient carrier is infinite; a maximal cluster need not be. Here specieslike means nonempty connected, ancestry-convex and IAP, without an infinite-set clause. Reflection forces infinitude only when a member has infinitely many descendants in the ambient graph.

There are two endpoints: every already admissible set extends at its fixed real Δ; and for every Δ≥0 each original organism lies in some such maximal set. The second, seed-existence statement uses nonnegative Δ. No finite-root premise or chronological numbering is assumed, and birthdate ties are allowed.

The accepted seed lemma constructs a connected convex reflecting IAP set through each organism by minimizing anchored convex reflecting sets under reverse inclusion; if IAP fails, its productive nondescendant region gives a strictly smaller such set. The intersection/reflection supplier proves the needed lower bounds, so neither a singleton nor an arbitrary descendant cone is assumed adequate. A common-ancestor seed satisfies every $\Delta\ge0$.

For an increasing chain of window-admissible sets, its union remains connected and convex. The real window bounds founders in a finite birth sublevel. The finite-founder directed-union lemma preserves IAP: an offending nondescendant region supplies a productive founder; reflection and convexity in a sufficiently large stage force arbitrarily many of its descendants there, contradicting that stage's IAP. The fixed real window and reflection also survive. Zorn therefore gives a maximal member containing the seed, against **all** competitors with that same $\Delta$. A faithful birth-order reindexing and its inverse transport dates and arbitrary competitors back to the original graph. No uniqueness, partition or effective biological classifier follows. Checked endpoint: `RealSpeciesTheorem.every_vertex_in_maximal_real_window`. The historical Nat-ID theorem now has a separately qualified arbitrary-carrier successor: finite strict REAL birth sublevels derive an ordered enumeration, and pull/push transports every competitor at the SAME duration. Its endpoints `FounderCarrier.every_vertex_in_maximal_real_window` and `FounderCarrier.maximal_window_extension` retain the nonnegative-duration condition only for per-organism existence. ## Population degree, pruning and schedules

**CB-POPULATION-001 — the exact binary child-cap threshold is two.** The source edge-labelled avoider has edges $w-1\to w$, $w-2\to w$, with complementary labels at each $w\ge2$. Pass to its directed line graph: a vertex is an original edge, permanently gendered by that edge's label; consecutive original edges give new edges. Encode positive ports by
$$
\operatorname{source}(x)=\lfloor x/2\rfloor,\qquad
\operatorname{target}(x)=\lfloor x/2\rfloor+1+(x\bmod2).
$$
Omit port0, the nonexistent original edge $0\to1$. Each retained port has exactly two children $2v,2v+1$, where $v$ is its target, and exactly three roots are ports1,2,3. At a nonroot, its incoming original edges supply both permanent genders. Paths lift/project with exactly the same first letter and phase. The underlying graph is fixed; only genders depend on the avoided word.

The inherited avoidance theorem handles every non-eventually-periodic binary word. All sufficiently late ports descend from any fixed port, so nondescendants are finite. Any infinite ancestrally closed subset must contain every port, proving inspecies; common late descendants give connectivity, and convexity/reflection follow. Conversely child cap below two contradicts the source degree-conservation theorem, while every eventually periodic word is realized in every eligible population. Thus an avoider exists iff cap $\ge2$ and the target is not eventually periodic. Three-root optimality is not claimed. [Exact cap proof](https://github.com/Sodelin/Work-on-Samuel-Alexander-Research-/blob/e2502c82ab9a77c00543932f775a71e5374221f7/notes/CAP-TWO-THRESHOLD.md), [species strengthening](https://github.com/Sodelin/Work-on-Samuel-Alexander-Research-/blob/e2502c82ab9a77c00543932f775a71e5374221f7/notes/CAP-TWO-SPECIES.md).

**CB-POPULATION-002 — critical tails.** For a simple naturally ordered population with $k$ required incoming labels and child cap $k$, after all roots the first $k$ future vertices at a cut need at least $k,k-1,\ldots,1$ incoming crossings. Hence crossing count is at least $k(k+1)/2$. If every sufficiently late cut attains it, no crossing can target later than those $k$ vertices. Apply this at $u+1$; every child of $u$ lies in $u+1,\ldots,u+k$. Equal neighbouring cut counts force zero degree defect and exactly $k$ children, filling all those positions. One isolated attaining cut gives no whole-tail rigidity.

Independently of minimum width, eventual degree conservation gives exactly $k$ in/out edges. Choose the actual finite crossing edges at one root-free cut as slots. At each birth, biject the $k$ incoming edges with the $k$ outgoing replacements in those same slots; all other slots retain their edges. The stored source/label tokens decode exactly all edges whose **target** is on the tail, including pre-tail sources. Every stored edge has a finite target, so every slot is eventually selected: fairness is derived. Classical finite bijections supply existence, not an oracle algorithm. If this full named-slot/replacement-label schedule is additionally eventually periodic, the accepted periodic-schedule lemma realizes every infinite word beginning within one period. Finite slots alone do not prove periodicity. [Exact encoding](https://github.com/Sodelin/Work-on-Samuel-Alexander-Research-/blob/e2502c82ab9a77c00543932f775a71e5374221f7/notes/PORT-ENCODING.md).

**CB-POPULATION-003 — productive core and optimal finite repair.** Start with the actual **infinite BinaryNatPopulation**, with finite roots, finite child sets and strictly increasing natural chronology. Retain vertices with infinitely many strict descendants. Finite roots and finite branching force an infinite productive core; it is ancestrally closed, so all original ancestors of a retained vertex are retained. Every infinite path stays productive; conversely every retained path is original. Thus the full infinite-word language and supplied child cap are preserved after faithful birth-order reindexing. Infinitely many isolated finite components would defeat the infinite-core conclusion without these hypotheses. Additional whole-core species claims require whole-graph IAP, and reflection-constrained maximality is not silently replaced by unrestricted descendant closure.

For a fixed infinite retained set $S$, call a vertex deficient if it lacks some required incoming label from $S$. Every deletion-only eligible repair must make it a root, because deletion cannot supply a missing label. Finite roots therefore require finitely many deficiencies. Conversely delete **all** remaining incoming edges at deficient vertices and no others. This makes exactly those vertices roots and supplies all labels at every nonroot. Every permissible repair is a subrelation of this one: it is the greatest retained relation, with inclusion-smallest deletions/roots. Chronology bounds all deleted edges below one finite birth bound. Cofinite $S$ automatically has finite deficiency by finite children. Repaired paths preserve avoidance, not full language equality. The source's C0 repair disconnects, while C1 has the shifted original unlabelled geometry. [Exact repair theorem](https://github.com/Sodelin/Work-on-Samuel-Alexander-Research-/blob/e2502c82ab9a77c00543932f775a71e5374221f7/notes/BOUNDARY-REPAIR-THEOREM.md).

**CB-POPULATION-004 — state can beat a fixed static certificate.** In the specified synthetic dead/r/l cellular automaton, every valid phase-blind two-label local convex-hull certificate has east support exactly1, including the actual real hull intersection. For the same actual update, potential $p(r)=1,p(l)=0$ gives a charge strip preserved by the two-predecessor row identities. A finite nonempty configuration has attained minimum/maximum charge. If a later configuration is its translate, those extrema force horizontal displacement0. A two-cell oscillator verifies nonvacuity by complete configuration equality, including the dead exterior. This compares one actual rule against its stated **whole** static-certificate family; it is not a bound for a published binary Life-like rule. The later real-hull adapter is distinct from the original rational module receipt.

## Feedback: mixing, selection and finite populations

**CB-FEEDBACK-001.** In the declared finite-deme model, every organism disseminates through its own deme within one uniform lag; cohorts are occupied and same-deme parents are covered. Make the recurrent migration graph from edges occurring arbitrarily late. If an infinite set is eventually contained in one recurrent SCC, finite directed paths inside that SCC plus the uniform dissemination lag make every organism reaching it ancestor of all sufficiently late members; organisms not reaching it have only finitely many descendants there. Thus IAP holds. Conversely, infinitely many members outside one recurrent SCC permit a recurrent predecessor distinction; uniform local dissemination produces one organism with infinitely many descendants and nondescendants in the set. Hence every infinite IAP set is contained modulo finitely many members in one unique recurrent SCC. Whole-history IAP is recurrent strong connectivity. Sparse subsets need not equal the whole SCC. The common-cutoff component tail is connected, convex and reflecting. All dissemination/coverage hypotheses are essential.

**CB-FEEDBACK-002.** For $F(x)=3x^2-2x^3$, use the symmetric two-deme mixture with $0<m<1/8$. At polarized coordinates $((1+d)/2,(1-d)/2)$, fixed-point algebra is $(1-2m)d^2=1-6m$. Both diagonal derivatives are $F'=6m/(1-2m)<1$; the Jacobian eigenvalues are $F'$ and $(1-2m)F'$, proving local asymptotic stability in two dimensions. The polarized root is taken with 0<d<1. The rational witness $m=7/78,d=3/4$ is $(7/8,1/8)$. For cultural/neutral frequencies in [0,1], 0<g≤1/2 and k≥0, neutral exchange $\rho_t=g/(1+k(x_t-y_t)^2)$ lies between $a=g/(1+k)>0$ and $1/2$. Therefore its difference multiplies by $1-2\rho_t$, giving $|u_t-v_t|\le(1-2a)^t|u_0-v_0|\to0$. Cultural polarization and neutral homogenization can coexist in this one-way coupling.

**CB-FEEDBACK-003.** Start with a nonnegative unit-sum vector, a **constant selection coefficient s>0**, and q_n=x1(n)+x3(n). Choose r≥0 satisfying both initial odds inequalities x2≤r x1 and x4≤r x3. For the actual affinity recursion, set $p=x_1+x_2,D=x_1x_4-x_2x_3$,
$$
y_1=x_1-b_1D,\ y_2=x_2+b_1D,\
y_3=x_3+b_2D,\ y_4=x_4-b_2D.
$$
Transmission applies the same nonnegative matrix to $(x_1,x_3)$ and $(x_2,x_4)$. Thus coordinatewise odds bounds $x_2\le r x_1,x_4\le r x_3$ persist. Selection multiplies the B coordinates by $1+s$, all normalized by one positive mass, so the odds bound contracts to $r/(1+s)$. Induct with $r_n=r/(1+s)^n$; summing gives
$$
0\le1-q_n\le r_n/(1+r_n)\le r_n\to0.
$$
Choose $r$ as the maximum initial ratio when both B background coordinates are positive. Arbitrary generation-dependent affinities in $[0,1]$ are allowed. This proves convergence from the actual recursion, not just absence of an interior equilibrium. [Coefficient proof and source model](https://github.com/Sodelin/Work-on-Samuel-Alexander-Research-/blob/e2502c82ab9a77c00543932f775a71e5374221f7/research/feedback-speciation/FOGARTY-GLOBAL-RATE.md).

**CB-FEEDBACK-004.** In the exact one-diploid-per-deme weighted-pool model, construct the finite genotype/marker transition by selection, recombinant gamete probabilities and independent offspring draws; pulse before selection and normalize by $B_0=1/8$. The retained exact absorbing-kernel calculation gives genetic RI $937945083/2233177303$, below pure-reset RI $3/7$ by $19130904/2233177303$, with pulse-start marginal error $\le(99/100)^n$. The matched deterministic recurrence has genetic RI at least $1027087570805/2255346713174>3/7$ from biological generation4 onward, and its proved limit keeps that ordering. These rational certificates are named finite-kernel/recurrence suppliers, not recomputed experiments here. The reversal compares those exact matched life cycles; weighted migration is not integer migrant exchange, and terminal marker classes need not be singleton genotype states.

**CB-FEEDBACK-005.** Four independent uniform Fin14 draws per generation encode both the actual parent slots and marker homologues. Summing their fibres proves all 81 ordinary/nine pulse marginal identities and off-support closure of the finite count kernel. The **same raw draws** generate the parent process and the frozen-kernel marker path law. A specified resolving parent table has probability $36/2401$. Independent future generations, also conditional on any positive-mass past event, bound failure to resolve by $(2365/2401)^k$. For each organism born earlier, this tends to zero; countable union gives whole-history IAP almost surely. The marker RI remains $3/7$. Exact avoidance of a parent table and failure of ancestry resolution are not equated, nor are marker counts independent across generations.

**CB-FEEDBACK-006.** In the separate N-diploid integer-exchange model, a positive two-generation accessibility probability gives geometric fixation/loss tails for a nonmutating allele. At fixed finite N, long genetic burn-in therefore removes the marker barrier uniformly over later horizons. Complete reset from the correctly prepared selected background instead gives
$$
R=s(1-m)/(1-ms)
$$
for every allowed horizon and terminal fixation. This is N-independence at fixed $m=k/N$, not fixed migrant count. Genetic diversity $H=2\bar p(1-\bar p)$ has iterated limits0 with finite-N long burn-in first and $1/2$ with the infinite-census limit first. This is not a proved RI limit-interchange theorem. ## Genome information and ownership

**CB-GENOME-001.** Any finite acyclic Wong graph has two connected infinite topological completions preserving/reflection all old edges and paths. For one, feed every old terminal into a common future chain: every old vertex then has cofinite descendants, and every infinite ancestrally closed subset contains the entire prefix, giving the stated whole-population inspecies/maximal specieslike status. For the other, attach a common junction then two infinite forks; a fork member has infinitely many descendants and nondescendants, violating IAP. New vertices are later, so no new old-to-old ancestry appears. Dates and finite degrees are chosen accordingly. An algorithm reading only the common finite graph cannot return opposite required statuses. This is not equality of DNA likelihoods, extension of future interval annotations or a supplied genome-to-organism map.

**CB-GENOME-002.** Take 0≤c≤1. For the prescribed rooted three-taxon law $P(s)=1-2c/3,P(g\ne s)=c/3$, $c<1$, the strict maximum identifies s and then c. Coordinate error $\varepsilon$ preserves it if $2\varepsilon<1-c$; the half-gap is sharp. If $0<c\le1$, every finite ordered sample has positive product mass under every label, so a uniformly certain finite decoder is impossible. The singleton adverse sample has mass at least $(c/3)^n$, not by itself a total-error theorem. A separate Hoeffding/union calculation bounds error by $\min(1,2e^{-n(1-c)^2/2})$. The law is stipulated; no DNA or coalescent-source derivation is included.

**CB-GENOME-003.** One fixed eight-lane transmission graph supports two diploid owner schedules after every prescribed finite mixing prefix. Pairing lanes within two enduring lineages versus alternating the relevant cross-pairing gives opposite whole-population IAP/species/inspecies status, while every organism edge is witnessed by the same transmissions. Each has four founders/four organisms per generation, two copies, two distinct parents and two children, with the stated dates/connectivity. In this exact family, the lane0/lane3 co-ownership record distinguishes the eventual schedule iff sampled odd generations are unbounded. All even-generation records coincide. If switch times are bounded by supplied even B, one query at B+1 suffices and an odd sample after B is necessary. The infinite decoder is set-theoretic; ownership is input, not inferred from phasing, and one realized infinite history is not a probability-law collision.

**CB-GENOME-004.** Each child independently chooses a FAIR parental symbol in each block, independently across blocks; exact symbols distinguish disjoint parental pairs. Each actual family has m≥1 children and B≥1 blocks. Join children sharing any block symbol. Cross-family edges are impossible. For one family of m children, disconnection means exactly two complementary block bitstrings, inducing one nontrivial unordered child bipartition. For each such partition the chance per independent block is $2^{1-m}$, so for $B\ge1$
$$
P(\text{recover family})=1-(2^{m-1}-1)2^{-B(m-1)}.
$$
Those partition events are disjoint. Independent families give the product of these factors; singleton families contribute 1, and an empty collection has product 1. The formula is not applied at m=0. Repeating each child's one fair bit across all blocks keeps each full one-block marginal but leaves success $2^{1-m}$, regardless of B. Adding blocks only adds within-family edges, so pathwise a correct recovery cannot be spoiled even without independence. The theorem is a stated block model, not a general multigeneration DNA/pedigree decoder.

## Wong: actual interval records and their limits

**CB-WONG-001.** For active count $k\ge2$, split and merge hazards are $bk$ and $ak(k-1)/2$, with $a>0,b\ge0$. The actual count chain chooses up/down in those proportions; independent unit exponentials give waits divided by their sum. Its accepted birth-death hitting supplier proves finite absorption at one almost surely, including b=0 and zero-time n=1. Conditional on each actual recursively selected frontier, choose a uniform active lineage or unordered lineage pair and a normalized-Lebesgue cut in $(0,L)$. The recorder preserves stable IDs, full-genome partitions, coverage and event counts. Finite absorption plus finite positive waits yields a finite valid dated marked history almost surely. Reading count and timestamps from that stored history has exactly the constructed count/time law; no time is inferred from an undated graph. Malformed inputs fail explicitly. Immediate split/reunion can forget its raw cut under endpoint-local graph semantics, so not every raw history is invertible from a simplification. A finite expected physical-time bound is not assigned by this endpoint.

**CB-WONG-002.** At one coordinate, scan actual interval records to obtain the unique local parent function. Trace rootward from samples with fuel $N=|V|$; acyclicity bounds strict ancestor rank and proves sufficient fuel. Memoization preserves reference traversal, and returns exactly active edges whose child has a sample descendant, retaining unary ancestors/IDs. Equality with **all** original local edges is equivalent to sample support: every active edge's child is sample-ancestral. Repeated/unordered catalogue entries are harmless when list membership is correct. Abstract writes are $\le N$, checks/lookups $\le$ writes + sample-list length, without a constant-time implementation assumption. Root and unvisited entries can both be `none`, so repeated root lookup is allowed. This is an edge-relation round trip, not recovery of interval subdivision, dates, bytes or hidden histories.

**CB-WONG-003.** At each coordinate retain sample-ancestral edges below the reflexive common-ancestor boundary, then contract nonsample, nonbranching unary paths. Activity is constant on cells cut by the original finite endpoints, so these local relations assemble into one finite interval graph on the same IDs/samples, with no new breakpoints. Composition preserves sample-to-sample ancestry, and, when supplied, MRCA-to-sample paths. Unique MRCA existence is proved only with nonempty samples, unique local parents and some common ancestor. Without one, the construction is still total; empty/singleton samples produce no retained edges. Canonical storage merges touching intervals into unique strictly separated maximal intervals for each ordered edge; equal complete local relations give equal canonical graphs, and canonicalization is idempotent. This storage idempotence is not idempotence of the whole truncation/contraction pipeline. The classical global construction is not a reported executable exporter or probability-law equivalence.

**CB-WONG-004.** Let q be an onto finite-fibre owner map and compare saturated candidates $C=q^{-1}S$. Exact ancestry agreement for every pair of representatives with different owners makes rich descendant sets equal to owner-preimages up to the finite same-owner fibre. A set is finite iff its preimage is finite; thus IAP and reflection transfer. Sound paths give convexity pullback and common-ancestor descent; exact ancestry plus acyclicity gives convexity equivalence. Internal fibre connectivity supplies the reverse connectivity direction, hence specieslike equivalence and maximality among saturated candidates. Unrestricted rich maximality descends, but its converse is not claimed.

For the later IAP-only refinement, each representative's ancestry-answer error set inside C may merely be finite. Its descendant set differs finitely from the owner-preimage, so finite/cofinite alternatives are unchanged. The error bound may vary per representative; no agreement outside C is needed. Reflection, convexity and maximality do **not** follow under that weaker hypothesis. A connected fork and chain under identity ownership show that soundness alone is insufficient. None of this supplies an owner map or biological validity, and a finite sampled ARG cannot be onto an infinite organism history.
