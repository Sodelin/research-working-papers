## G1 — bounded core, unbounded words

**CB-G1-001.** A single originated decorated core can be chosen before all future legal samples, tuples, old-register contexts and continuations. It preserves the stated completed unranked forest/register/root-history response. Its retained hybrids, vertices and edges satisfy
$$
h\le2n-2,\qquad V\le6n-5,\qquad E\le8n-8;
$$
the bounds are sharp for every $n\ge4$. Actual hidden serial words remain unbounded.

**Argument.** Delete underlying bridges. The component quotient is a tree, directed away from the root: a root-to-vertex path cannot cross a bridge backwards. Every nonroot component has one entry. A hybrid child is a distinct outgoing bridge, so a nonroot $p$-port component has at most $p-1$ hybrids; the root has at most $p$. A nonroot two-port cyclic component therefore has exactly one hybrid; binary indegree/outdegree counting leaves no intervening branching vertex, so it is a bigon. Suppress only those components into actual serial decorations; retain the root blob and all branching blobs. The root has at least two ports by LSA. In the remaining bridge tree, summing $p-2$ gives $n-2$; the port bounds then give $2n-2$ retained hybrids. Binary degree identities give $V=2n+2h-1$, $E=2n+3h-2$.

For sharpness, use a two-hybrid, two-exit root component and repeatedly substitute the same two-hybrid branching component at an exit. Each substitution adds one taxon and two retained hybrids. Its directed internal edges are $v_0v_1,v_0H_A,v_1v_2,v_1H_B,v_2H_A,v_2H_B$, with cut children at the two hybrids; the root version has no entry, later versions have one entry. This yields $h=2n-2$, is binary, outer-labelled, cut-child and LSA-rooted, and has no suppressible nonroot two-port component. Positive parameters may be chosen throughout.

Preservation is physical, not a replacement by arbitrary tables: keep each suppressed component's full original serial word and its original-primitive provenance. At a cut boundary its exact kernel is unchanged. Compose these equalities with opaque grafting and the same exterior programme; this proves all future permitted contextual laws with one decoration. Internal merger times or arbitrary extra controls are not silently added to the preserved readout.

## G3 — one finite positive source, or none

### The exact criterion

**CB-G3-001.** A supplied finite graph's ordinary topology rows compile to polynomials in survivals and inheritance variables. Kingman transitions use powers $x^{\binom m2}$; routing is a finite sum of products of coins; finite ancestral completion has rational coefficients. COMMON bits are processed once. All rows use one tuple, so strict real-algebraic feasibility decides a fixed-graph fit. Enumerating admitted finite graphs semidecides YES for finite rational/algebraic inputs.

For a full computably presented response profile, let $\mathcal C_B$ be the finite union of images of all graphs with at most $B$ hybrids, using the closed parameter slab $[1/B,1-1/B]^d$ and all legal supplied ID maps. Let $T(B,m)$ require one such source to fit the first $m$ coordinates within a prescribed rational error tending to zero. Then
$$
\text{one finite strict realizer exists}
\iff \exists B\ \forall m\ T(B,m).
$$
Necessity chooses a budget covering the actual finite source and all its strict parameters. For sufficiency choose witnesses as $m\to\infty$; one of finitely many graphs repeats, its slab is compact, and a convergent subsequence fits each coordinate by continuity. The limit is still strict. Negation is $\forall B\exists m\neg T(B,m)$, not a terminating search over budgets.
