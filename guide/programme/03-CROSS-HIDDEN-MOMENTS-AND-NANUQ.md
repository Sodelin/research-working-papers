## NANUQ: what the distance actually records

This is the connected programme Nolan referred to as “NANDQ”. Its source is the pinned finite binary semidirected, LSA-rootable, outer-labelled planar **galled** class on at least four taxa, with original cut/parallel-bigon conventions; galled does not mean level one. Its oracle returns **sets of distinct displayed resolved quartet topologies**, not switching multiplicities or gene-tree probabilities. The theorem uses the order of any chosen original outer embedding, not an independently chosen local circle.

For distinct $x,y$, let $\rho(x,y;p,q)$ be the fraction of distinct displayed topologies on $x,y,p,q$ that separate $x,y$. The original raw distance is
$$
d_N(x,y)=2n-4+2\sum_{\{p,q\}\subset X\setminus\{x,y\}}\rho(x,y;p,q),
\qquad d_N(x,x)=0.
$$
The original arithmetic averaging convention is essential.
