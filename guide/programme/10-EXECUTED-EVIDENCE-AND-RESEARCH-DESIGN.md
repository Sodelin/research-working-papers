## Cross-scale research: compact elementary content and the untested proposal

**CB-CROSSSCALE-001.** Sodelin's missed-scope/shadow hypothesis distinguishes an intended useful outcome from success at a narrower registered task. Let V(D) be the **admitted compatible-model** set, C_A(D) the targets/proposals a procedure A can reach, and R_q(D)={q(θ):θ∈V(D)} the target-response set. These sets are different: failure to search outside C_A(D) says nothing about whether V(D) itself forces q.

The exact elementary statements are:

- A target q is identified by observation o iff o(θ)=o(θ′) implies q(θ)=q(θ′). In that case define q̄(o(θ))=q(θ); the fibre condition proves this is well-defined, hence q=q̄∘o. Conversely any such factorization gives fibre constancy.
- Adding a correct view intersects compatibility fibres. Postprocessing a view enlarges them. Additional views cannot remove a true source if their contracts are correct, but a wrong label or incompatible model assumption can.
- A memoryless update on a representation r exists iff r(θ)=r(θ′) implies r(F_aθ)=r(F_aθ′) for every allowed action a, with readouts factoring too. Define the quotient update by this condition. A single same-r/different-next-r pair refutes it. History-dependent changing views require the corresponding history/sufficient-state closure, not just current visual similarity.
- If each different-target pair in a **finite** candidate set has a permitted separating test, choose one per pair to obtain a finite separating family. Compactness without a uniform separated/open-cover supplier does not automatically extend this to every infinite class or executable equality test.

These are standard factorization/observability ideas with their source roles explicit; they are not proof of universal self-similarity, model internals or a scientific mechanism. The proposed intervention examines decisive dependencies, generates a consequential stronger target/representation and seeks a discriminating test. Comparing it against ordinary execution and generic auditing under matched total budgets remains a proposed empirical evaluation; no such experiment occurred.

## Reading evidence without changing its meaning

An exact certificate states an inequality and its admitted inputs. A replay checks a selected implementation against saved data. A hand proof establishes a quantified mathematical statement at its assumptions. A Lean receipt additionally binds an elaborated statement to its actual dependencies and axioms. Source authentication binds bytes, not truth; a formal component can still lack a biological observation adapter. This distinction is why a concise library records both the argument and its source/verification boundary.
