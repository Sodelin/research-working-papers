## Bounded structure is useful without bounding the full inverse problem

For its admitted n≥4 taxon class and original registry, G1 chooses one source-derived core with h≤2n−2, V≤6n−5 and E≤8n−8. The same decorated core works across the theorem's later observation/sample/control quantifiers. The all-size sharpness is simultaneous for those three core bounds. Choosing a new core after each observation would be a weaker statement.

The decoration can retain positive finite kernel words and provenance of arbitrarily large hidden parts. Core cardinalities therefore do not supply a full realizing-source size bound, terminating G3 recognition or arbitrary ranked-trajectory preservation. Its stated preservation is contextual and unranked with the specified root/calendar history, not every internal time or outside observation. This identifies a real reusable interface while isolating the remaining inverse-source obligation.

The G2 selected-panel endpoint is a pushforward equality under literal pruning of one source's registered timed readout. It retains the joint original register, including the controlled-mask convention. Equality after forgetting ages does not imply timed equality. Natural/fixed-mask sampling and data-dependent panel selection require different assumptions; exchanging correlated register coordinates for independent marginals changes the experiment.

The separate finite transition theorem states QP=PR and A_aP=PB_a for each declared pulse exactly when all nonnegative-time operator words intertwine. This is established finite-state semigroup algebra with source-specific binding, not a novel operator theorem or permission to reorder physical events. Equality at a single terminal summary is weaker. A general controlled history needs the actual conditional-law/policy adapter, as in the separate controlled-state and statistical-decision maps.

## MSci: a newer analytic check, with the process bridge still open

The fixed model uses backbone ((A,B),C), one backward B-to-C pulse at 0<h<t1<t0, 0<g<1, five positive pair rates with the stated same-population ties across the pulse, two phased labelled copies per species, and a known stationary homogeneous JC channel. Both homologous sites in a locus share one genealogy; loci are independent replicates under the declared law. The nine features are AC1, AC2, CC1, BC1, BC2, AB1, AB2, AA1 and BB1. This identifies numerical parameters within that family; it does not infer the assumed topology/direction or solve every MSci ambiguity.

The older selected guides describe reviewed hand results and no Lean endpoint for their universal claim. **Newer evidence changes that analytic-formula status.** The public [31 October qualification](../manuscripts/msci/evidence/LEAN-ANALYTIC-QUALIFICATION-20261031.md) reports the closed named result: admitted p,q with equal `MSCi.TwoSite.nineRawFeatures` are equal. It reports 18 selected modules, 16 freshly compiled and two previously qualified providers, with the named standard-axiom audit. This assessment read the public qualification body, not every raw audit or imported proof object, and performed no compilation. It does not upgrade the separately stated Jacobian, interval contractor or full observation process to the same checked status.

The actual six-copy current-block process and same-genealogy two-site JC observation still require a checked connection to the analytic feature formulas. The [current source](../manuscripts/msci/evidence/PULSE-STRICT-CALENDAR-OBSTRUCTION.md) also analyzes a restricted direct pulse encoding: a strict calendar, one hybrid child and an admitted descendant-path invariant obstruct one proposed immediate donor/recipient graph interpretation. That is a source-specific conditional hand argument, not a theorem that every pulse encoding is impossible. A different augmented graph, pulse-capable process or approximation construction requires its own proof and source admission; a namespace match or custom boundary kernel is insufficient.

The exact inverse and its pointwise nonsingular Jacobian do not provide uniform conditioning, narrow finite-data localization or an efficient executable. The contractor is a conservative all-compatible-source inclusion and may make no progress. The retained interval-summary witness forces normalized r_A width at least 2/11 in one frozen box although a 1/20 target was sought. The two full DNA laws are not claimed equal; this is an obstruction for that summary/cover, not for every method or future data. A source compiler, completed process bridge and admitted sample law remain substantive work.

The published BPP/MSci discussion distinguishes topology-only observations from timed genealogies and asks about alignment-versus-timed-genealogy identifiability with multiple samples/sites. That is the relevant external question. The retained bounded known-clock bridge is a restricted candidate response; it is not an unrestricted solution covering recombination, unknown clocks or arbitrary networks. ## Histories must use one coherent hidden-name alignment

The bounded demographic-history result has its own class: positive-duration constant-rate epochs, independent current-block stochastic routing, bounded population/epoch counts and an ancestral root, with excluded migration, instantaneous mergers and lineage creation. Its local theorem gives a finite list of rate-compatible boundary alignments. Independently selecting one valid permutation per boundary does not necessarily match the full genealogy law.

The later hand-reviewed global result instead uses the same hidden-epoch permutation on both adjoining boundaries, and claims an iff with coherent history equivalence. Its legal labelled-forest realization is the source-specific step; generic invariant separation and positive finite moments are prior methods. The explicit balanced-panel bound is finite and extremely large, not a practical sequencing recommendation. Exact rational/algebraic equality tests and arbitrary computable-real equality have different algorithmic contracts. A stricter separated event alphabet can remove some label ambiguities; simultaneous pulse factorizations remain different targets. The finite known-clock sequence bridge also needs the correct whole-panel quantifier, not a collection of unrelated marginal identifications.

## NANUQ: keep three different meanings of a quartet separate

The retained hand Theorem A concerns the original mean over **distinct displayed quartet topologies**, not uniform switching multiplicity or a coalescent quartet probability. For its outer-labelled planar galled LSA-rootable class it claims circularity and positive split support equal to the union of displayed-tree splits, without a finite-level bound. Correct source/cap representation and global lifting are dependencies, not consequences of the finite enumeration alone.

Holtgrefe and colleagues pose the multi-blob level-two and level-three-bloblet extensions in Section 6. If the retained proof and convention translations hold, Theorem A covers those structural cases. The local parametric result is narrower than a maximal global family. A published multiplicity-weighted circular metric is different; it must receive credit without being substituted for the original distinct-topology question. The article also distinguishes structural inference from a future noisy-data algorithm. This assessment read the primary question section, not an entire proof or worldwide current-status review. [Primary article](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11538-025-01549-4).

The selected 53-source formal chain has concrete source-composition, cap restriction, opened-choice and curve endpoints. It still leaves source embedding/admission, Jordan/domain transport, faces/contours, adjacent-tip order, counting and positive split-support/global geometry obligations. An injective circle trace is not already a Jordan separation/outer-face theorem. Historical selected guarded compilations, reused providers, the all-level hand argument and complete end-to-end formalization have different evidentiary scopes. This does not retract the hand claim, but it prevents calling the whole claim Lean-closed.

The retained strict half-unit distance rounding result is likewise conditional on a correct structural theorem, normalization, a supplied correct order and an actual error promise. It does not supply a statistical estimator or sequence model. Exact support may fail when outer planarity is removed even if the resulting distance remains circular. Neither a split union nor surjective choice reindexing preserves joint tree occurrence frequencies automatically.

## A small formal supplier still needs the stochastic adapter

The G2 pair-age reconstruction proves fixed-shape decoration injectivity and measurable decoding from all ordered pairs of actual leaves, with separate pruning compatibility. An unknown tree shape is not an input it recovers; equal graft ages can leave different shapes with the same off-diagonal pair matrix absent additional conditions. This is a reconstructed checked component, not byte recovery of the old missing historical file. Its corrected later strict comparison does not erase an earlier failed inventory comparison. Source membership in an aggregate and a stochastic timed-law theorem remain distinct acceptance checks.
