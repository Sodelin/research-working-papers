# Physics question registry: separate learning, certification and access

The retained physics questions have different targets and access models. This Codex/OpenAI source assessment identifies where existing connection maps can contribute and where the required physical theorem is absent. It does not introduce a quantum algorithm, prove a preprint, run a model or claim human review. Original source attribution and scientific ownership remain unchanged.

| External task | Retained potential contribution | What is not supplied |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Learn an unknown local thermal state efficiently | Observation-target and confidence-set contracts can help specify a finite target and its uncertainty | A full-state trace-norm learning algorithm efficient in all charged parameters, candidate description/selection and measurement implementation |
| Local Gibbs-state certification | Existing PGM/risk-to-test and resource maps state concrete conditional or completed bounded results | The missing size/prior/candidate-uniform interacting-Gibbs risk supplier and full optimum rate |
| Learning through restricted probes and controls | Controlled-history and G7 resource ideas help expose legal actions, history dependence and costs | A source-faithful quantum state/control/readout adapter, routing analysis, realistic pulse/noise theorem or a direct import of exact biological rank deletion |
| Unstructured Hamiltonian certification with forward dynamics access | A source-status comparison prevents pursuing an already negatively answered unrestricted scaling question | No new negative answer from the retained work; the external preprint's access, distance and precision regimes must remain intact |

The retained physics guide compares a thermal-copy state-learning theorem with a different certification theorem. Its state-learning baseline has inefficient net-search postprocessing; a confidence-fibre formulation does not make that search efficient or produce the unknown state. Earlier Hamiltonian-learning access results likewise cannot be substituted for full-state learning solely because both concern temperature or a Hamiltonian. These remain source/target/resource translations to establish, not generic “connections” that solve the consumer.

The single-bright-qubit direction in the guide assumes known graph/backbone structure, a nonzero lower reference coupling, ideal rapid control across the register and its specified reset/readout access. Probe placement, routing congestion and physical finite-duration controls are distinct further questions. A one-readout description must not erase those control powers. A lower bound for experiments with one uninterrupted evolution does not automatically apply to an interleaved-control protocol. The retained exact biological G7 theorem has yet another source/response/cost contract, so it supplies a formulation analogy until an actual quantum adapter is proved.

One status correction is concrete. Escudero Gutiérrez, Lee and Zur's v2 preprint describes its Corollary 4.4 as a negative answer to the earlier unrestricted O(1/ε) certification direction. For n≥7 and 0<ε<1/8 it states a forward-evolution lower bound Ω(min{ε⁻²,2^(n/2)ε⁻¹}) at success at least 9/10, in normalized Frobenius distance. The minimum cannot be erased at fixed n and arbitrarily fine precision. The abstract/version record and selected formal statement were read; the proof and external acceptance were not independently audited. [Primary v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.03205v2), [version record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03205).

This negative answer concerns unrestricted alternatives under dynamics access. It neither contradicts a result promising local alternatives nor settles thermal-copy state learning or the retained Gibbs-testing supplier question. A new outreach/research plan should preserve that task separation and credit the external authors. Existing relevant authors have already been contacted in the agreed lane; this note reserves no new recipient and requests no replay.

Useful remaining work is to choose a single physical task and supply its exact source class, legal operations, observation law, output metric, error/confidence guarantees and charged resources. Then compare each retained supplier against those premises, explicitly returning an unmet premise when necessary. The existing [quantum](QUANTUM-CONNECTIONS.md), [quantum-loss](QUANTUM-LOSS-CONNECTIONS.md), [oracle](QUANTUM-ORACLE-RESOURCES.md), [statistical-resource](STATISTICAL-RESOURCES.md) and [controlled-state](CONTROLLED-STATE.md) maps provide the current scoped results; this registry connects the question labels to those deliverables without promoting a biological theorem into a quantum implementation.

The selected underlying guide identity and primary-source observation scope are recorded in the collection provenance. Other physics preprint comparisons here are retained-guide reports, not fresh full-paper reviews or an exhaustive current-status survey.
