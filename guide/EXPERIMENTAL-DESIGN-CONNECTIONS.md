## A general exact-law design procedure at a finite declared interface

A complete registry of exactly r original hybrid IDs makes the original graph census finite under the source's binary conventions: V=2n+2r−1 and E=2n+3r−2. This is not a bound for an unknown source with arbitrarily many unregistered hybrids. Admission still needs its directed, LSA, hybrid-child bridge and outer-face conditions, including indexed parallel arcs.

For finite unranked readouts and allowed affine randomized programmes, a joint polynomial forest compiler supplies base coordinates z and programme responses B(a)z+b(a). One graph and parameter bank explain the entire history. Correlated records cannot be replaced by products of marginal laws. Extra rate/calendar constraints must be preserved in the actual encoding.

Under exact-real sign/algebraic-selection and deletion-closed trajectory costs, the accepted hand result gives a terminating mathematical procedure for the feasible integer-budget Pareto frontier, with an identifying semialgebraic policy or a response-consistent admitted-source lower certificate. A losing history retains conflicting sources fitting the **whole transcript**; one fixed source pair defeating every policy is not required.

The rank argument makes at most D physical calls informative when z has D coordinates. An exact-law call already in the span of previous observations can be supplied virtually. Reset/deletion closure is essential: skipping a call must neither invalidate later actions nor increase cost. This does not bound virtual computation uniformly, and repeated noisy measurements can reduce uncertainty despite algebraic redundancy. It also does not optimize expected unbounded stopping cost, installed library size or irreversible action sequences.

The available historical one-further-call producer has a narrower supplied-menu/selector interface and returns UNKNOWN on unsupported inputs or backend/resource failures. Its same-backend checks are not a deployed general Pareto-policy engine or a native/formal refinement proof.

## Two explicit optima, different observation menus

With exact unrooted quartet laws, natural routing plus a labelled forced marker gives the stated worst-case optimum: r touched sites, two positive-support configurations and one programme when its support cap L≥2, or ceil(2/L) programmes for L≥1. This covers the stated independent/unknown-mode branch with r≥1,n≥4; known COMMON has a separate passive optimum. Retaining the row label allows the conditional laws to be recovered from their known positive weights. Pooling away that label is a different experiment. One programme here packages two environments, not one locus or a free physical assay.

The static full-forcing-only menu instead requires all four bit patterns on every pair of original IDs. For r≥2 its optimal configuration count is

`C(r)=min {m≥4 : r≤binom(m−1,floor(m/2)−1)}`,

with ceil(C(r)/L) programmes under the declared support cap. Its r=0 and r=1 cases are separate. The binary strength-two covering-array optimum is classical: Choi, Kim and Oh credit Rényi, Katona, and Kleitman and Spencer for its solution. It is not a new combinatorial discovery here. The retained source-specific contribution is the admitted numerical-law collision outside a missing two-ID cylinder, which supports the lower bound for this exact experiment. A combinatorial missing pattern alone would not establish that source collision. The lower result is static and must not be promoted to an adaptive lower bound without its own transcript argument. [Classical prior, Choi–Kim–Oh](https://arxiv.org/html/1111.0587v1).

## A different useful receiver: complete quartet-support queries

The structural learner asks for the complete set of distinct displayed resolved topologies on four taxa. It does not receive one sampled gene tree or a concordance-factor vector. Its retained source chain gives Θ(n log n) deterministic adaptive query complexity, conditional on the named structural and split-count suppliers. The supplied-order sparse decoder and the order learner are distinct components; no circle is supplied in the full reconstruction problem.

The exact finite results Q*(4)=1 and Q*(5)=5 use admitted graph witnesses and universal upper bounds. The proposed all-size exact-minimum Bellman procedure additionally needs a complete bounded actual-source census. That general optimizer was not executed, and no Q*(n) above five is supplied. An asymptotic information bound is not the exact finite minimum or a near-linear runtime guarantee.

A confidence wrapper can abstain unless the possible complete-mask set is a singleton. A definite mask containing two topologies differs from uncertainty between two masks. Its error union bound controls completed-but-wrong output under the provider's specified guarantees; high completion probability requires a stronger ambiguity guarantee. Finite absence of a topology does not itself implement the complete-support oracle.

## Connections to causal design and quantified real computation

Addanki and coauthors study interventions on observed causal variables with conditional-independence information. Their strong separation condition requires the two directed bit patterns 01 and 10. G7 full-forcing pair coverage requires all four. Thus the predicates are related but differ, as do the actions and targets. Their approximation result cannot be copied to hybrid-parent intervention costs. [Primary Lemma 3.1 and model](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v119/addanki20a/addanki20a.pdf).

Akbari, Etesami and Kiyavash minimize intervention cost for a specified causal effect in a given graph, using a hitting-set connection. Their union-of-interventions lemma has single-c-component and target-avoidance premises. It does not justify collapsing distinct G7 parent assignments into one action. [Primary Lemma 1 and intervention model](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v162/akbari22a/akbari22a.pdf).

A supplied fixed semialgebraic design can be expressed as an existential design followed by a universal pair of source parameters. Conditional on the cited OpenAI manuscript's claimed counting-hierarchy theorem and a correct complete polynomial-circuit reduction, that **fixed design question** inherits its classification. This is not a practical runtime guarantee, witness producer or classification of the unrestricted adaptive game. The adaptive history recursion has additional alternating choices. Logarithms, variable exponentials and arbitrary real constants are not admitted integer polynomial circuits merely by being named. ## The remaining useful work is explicit

These results identify three receivers: exact-law policy selection with an admitted finite registry; static configuration design through source-backed covering arrays; and structural quartet recovery with a justified support oracle. Turning them into an actual system requires the right census and joint compiler, reliable algebraic/history selectors, a checked policy representation and its source semantics, and an admitted observation provider. Finite-data design additionally needs uncertainty, dependence, attainable controls and the correct statistical cost objective.

Historical selected Lean game/rank/calendar components and finite Python controls have narrower scopes than the full producer. Expected stopping constants, unknown extra hybrids and different physical action/cost models remain separate questions. The related causal literature supplies prior methods and comparison targets, not an established reduction solving those extensions.
