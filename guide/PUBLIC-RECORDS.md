# Existing public problem records

These links lead to existing VibeMathed records associated with this project's public sources. They offer another way to find questions, proof links and verification notes. The descriptions below are navigation aids; each record's statement and source-specific caveats control. A listing is not journal publication, independent human peer review, or proof of a priority claim. The linked records' classifications and notes may change.

- [Clock-JC transfer for finite MSci models](https://vibemathed.com/problem/flouri-et-al-msci-identifiability-transfer-under-the-homogeneous-clock-jc-observ): the stated long-locus observation transfer, distinct from the two-site fixed-family paper in this repository.
- [Thue–Morse first-occurrence formulas](https://vibemathed.com/problem/thue-morse-all-three-joshi-rust-first-occurrence-formulas): exact formulas for stated families, with an exponent exception and a source-specific formal package.
- [Power-free morphism lengths](https://vibemathed.com/problem/the-8-3-plus-stability-conjecture-for-power-free-binary-morphism-lengths): a proposed admissible-length result in the stated threshold range, with a separate strict-endpoint comparison.
- [Period-doubling palindromic length](https://vibemathed.com/problem/period-doubling-prefix-palindromic-length-is-not-2-regular): a proposed nonregularity result for the specified sequence; the written argument remains subject to specialist review.
- [All-level NANUQ support](https://vibemathed.com/problem/all-level-nanuq-circularity-and-exact-displayed-split-support): a proposed structural result for the particular admitted network class and original distinct-topology distance.
- [Universal families of avoiding populations](https://vibemathed.com/problem/no-countable-universal-family-of-avoiding-populations-even-with-exact-parent-cou): an obstruction for countable host families under the specified injective-embedding interpretation.
- [Ordinal certificates and pruning](https://vibemathed.com/problem/ordinal-certificates-and-exact-pruning-characterize-sequence-realization): a source-specific formalization of classical well-founded and finite-branching methods. The record includes a request to reassess novelty and listing eligibility.
- [A fixed founder-window condition](https://vibemathed.com/problem/maximal-specieslike-clusters-with-a-fixed-real-founding-window): maximality within a fixed-window mathematical class, without an empirical species claim.
- [Matching heights in an avoiding population](https://vibemathed.com/problem/exact-thue-morse-matching-heights-in-an-avoiding-population): a target-dependent matching-height question for the stated graph and coding.

The original site records are maintained by VibeMathed; their classifications are the site's own. The project's AI contributions and any reported formal checks are identified in the linked records and underlying sources. The records can overlap, use established mathematics, or describe partial results; they should not be counted as distinct novel discoveries merely because they have separate pages. This selection is not a complete inventory of the research.
