# Quantum decoding and estimation: three usable interfaces

This extends [the quantum connection map](QUANTUM-CONNECTIONS.md) using existing retained hand arguments. Attribution remains dot (OpenAI), working with Sodelin. AI reviews and exact controls are scoped evidence, not human endorsement or formal verification.

1. **Ordinary versus tilted PGM.** [Wilde's tilted-state decoder](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.15955v1) is a different measurement from the ordinary equal-prior PGM in the [retained three-candidate counterexample](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/27b143c73a4ac272b560b2974d83ff96df738531/research/2026-10-19-p1-distance-weighted-pgm-route/P1-DISTANCE-WEIGHTED-PGM-ROUTE-CHECK-20261019-v3.md). The counterexample disproves a proposed far-only root-fidelity bound for ordinary PGM with overlapping acceptable outputs, even for faithful two-local fixed-temperature Gibbs states, with all reservoir spins counted. Always returning the middle candidate has zero-copy loss in that example. Thus it is neither a decision lower bound nor a contradiction of Wilde's theorem. A useful follow-on is to find a quantitatively controlled loss-aware measurement, with no assumed full Gibbs rate.

2. **A reusable quadratic estimation supplier.** The [square-root-state application](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/27b143c73a4ac272b560b2974d83ff96df738531/research/2026-10-19-p1-distance-weighted-pgm-route/actual-distortion/square-root-comparison/P1-SQUARE-ROOT-PGM-COMPARISON-20261019.md) explicitly applies Albarelli–Branford–Rubio's established quadratic Bayesian PGM principle and known affinity/fidelity inequalities. It uses the same n-copy measurement and one common competing estimator for every square-root coordinate. Expected actual PGM squared Bures loss is at most four times optimal state-valued Bayes Bures risk; full trace squared loss is at most sixteen times that risk. A [scoped AI review](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/blob/27b143c73a4ac272b560b2974d83ff96df738531/research/2026-10-19-p1-distance-weighted-pgm-route/actual-distortion/square-root-comparison/P1-SQUARE-ROOT-PGM-INDEPENDENT-REVIEW-20261019.md) checks this application. This is not a novelty claim for the quadratic principle. The uniform interacting Gibbs risk supplier remains missing.

**Related correction to review.** Barnum and Knill published a 2026 [erratum](https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0325563) to their 2002 fidelity paper. Their [revised author preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0004088v2) states that it corrects an error affecting the extension of the original proof to ensembles of more than one state. The square-root-state application above credits the Albarelli–Branford–Rubio formulation; its relationship to the corrected Barnum–Knill proof has not been independently checked. The correction is a review lead, not by itself a finding that the retained application is invalid.

3. **A precise positive-score admission test.** [Albarelli–Chessa–Rubio](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.28070v1) require positive score self-convolution, stronger than entrywise nonnegativity. The hard score of a three-point chain of acceptable pairs is K=[[1,1,0],[1,1,1],[0,1,1]]. For v=(1,-2,1), vᵀKv=-2, so the score fails the necessary positive-semidefinite test. For the exponential of the corresponding zero-one loss, replacing the endpoint zero by t=exp(-κ) gives vᵀK_tv=2(t-1)<0 for κ>0. These are guide-derived algebraic comparisons awaiting specialist review. They identify a failed direct use of the hypothesis, not a flaw in the source or a ban on positive surrogates. Such a surrogate still needs a quantitative transfer to the desired full-trace loss and a uniform Gibbs bound.
