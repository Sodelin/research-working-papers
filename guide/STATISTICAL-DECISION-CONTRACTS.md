They are not a general solution of scientific inference. This source synthesis is authored by Codex/OpenAI; original sources retain their own attribution and AI-review/formal evidence. ## Exact identification is a contract, not a recovery score

The observation-foundations selection has 85 declarations including definitions, support lemmas and examples; its audit establishes no external open-problem closure. Agreement on a larger exact probe design narrows observational equivalence. Applying a common function to identical observations cannot restore a distinction. A target may nevertheless be identifiable without the whole state being identifiable: it need only be constant on each nonempty observation fibre.

The associated decoder is a mathematical correspondence, not automatically an algorithm or an assembled Lean import between the foundations and Semi packages. Range and total decoders have different nonemptiness hypotheses. Exact repeated constraints do not change a consistency set, whereas independent repeated noisy measurements can add information. None of these elementary facts establishes a clinical, social or biological effect. Historical lead holds are not released by a generic observation lemma.

## Transfer one law and one simulator, with the correct quantifiers

For experiments E=(P_θ) and F=(Q_θ), directional deficiency is inf_K sup_θ TV(KP_θ,Q_θ), with **one** ordinary Markov kernel K independent of θ. A state-dependent family of simulators is a different object. In the retained dominated/standard-Borel contract, one optimal kernel is stated to exist and transfers risks of losses bounded by M within M times deficiency. This is an abstract existence and risk statement, not a computable inverse for each sample.

The undominated and arbitrary-measurable cases keep distinct hypotheses: an abstract transition need not have an ordinary measurable kernel representation, and zero infimum need not imply exact attainment in every category. The retained non-Borel example demonstrates that distinction under its arbitrary parameter indexing. Its use is to prevent a false representation inference, not to establish that all scientific models are pathological.

Version discipline matters for external use. The dominated review names earlier bytes that were not recovered in the selected source history. The final source reports a prior-work-only update with unchanged proof sections; that is an author report rather than a verified byte comparison. Other path/answer and compact-certificate reviews match their specified retained proof identities. Those records should not be collapsed into a claim that every final proof has an exact-hash independent review or a Lean build.

The finite compact certificate offers a more concrete receiver. Given an admitted compact parameter class, a true finite r-cover, finite alphabets, valid TV moduli and simultaneous grid-law errors ρ_P,ρ_Q, exact primal/dual witnesses give a returned kernel with full-class upper error at most U+ρ_P+ρ_Q+ω_P(r)+ω_Q(r), capped at one. The corresponding lower bound is max(0,L−ρ_P−ρ_Q). The upper guarantee belongs to the **same returned kernel**, rather than another optimizer chosen afterward. A positive lower certificate gives an actual decision disadvantage under its premises.

The prototype is Python with rational witness checking; numerical optimization can propose candidates. A conservative fallback may be sound but loose. Cover geometry, scientific law access and sampling validity remain external premises. Adaptive table reuse requires a valid shared confidence event; a fixed-stage certificate is not automatically anytime-valid. A discrete target changing across a connected parameter boundary cannot be assigned a vanishing Dirac-law TV modulus there. No native deployment or generic infinite-alphabet optimizer follows.

## Finite samples, whole histories and stopping answer different questions

For [zero versus IID Bernoulli(e)](../manuscripts/calibration/MANUSCRIPT.md), with known 0<e<1 and n complete observations, let q=(1−e)^n. The retained sharp minimax maximum error is q/(1+q), while equal-prior Bayes error is q/2. Their decision rules differ. At every finite n there remains a positive all-zero ambiguity; for fixed positive e the error tends to zero as n increases. Allowing arbitrarily small positive rates removes a uniform fixed-n guarantee below 1/2. This is calibration under a specified model, not a conclusion that an unobserved scientific mechanism is impossible.

The separate full-history comparison needs actual conditional laws. Equal one-time marginals do not give equal path laws. Under the retained uniform-at-every-common-history error premises, finite-prefix TV is bounded by one minus the product of the one-step success factors. Consistency and a common history process matter; separately chosen finite-prefix simulators need not assemble into one causal simulator. A hidden-state posterior lift under an adaptive policy remains an additional controlled-memory bridge.

The stopping source uses one rule, one seed law independent of the stream, and IID reads under the same PMF. Positive-by-time events must respect an earlier negative first stop. The probability of eventual positive stopping is a supremum of continuous finite-prefix probabilities and is lower semicontinuous; it need not be continuous. Small finite-prefix errors therefore do not create an automatic exact negative decision.

Its closure obstruction and finite-alphabet strict-gap counterpart concern specified corruption classes. The positive construction is a guide derivation awaiting independent review and uses a known positive gap plus distance to a source-image set. Mathematical availability of that set is not an effective distance oracle. No Rust stopping engine or full biological assembly is supplied by the chosen packet.

## A precise external connection, with its scope left intact

Ramdas's *A complete characterization of sequential testability and change detectability in i.i.d. models* claims a broad power-one characterization. Its Proposition 3.17 specializes countable discrete alphabets to alternatives outside the null's ℓ¹ closure, with two-observation scores. Finite-alphabet TV closure is the corresponding setting for the retained comparison. The preprint separately identifies structural questions involving noncompact uncountable models and dependent or controlled observations. The retained finite-IID result does not settle those extensions. The primary record and selected proposition/remark were read here; the full proof and current human acceptance were not audited. [Primary v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.05752v1), [version record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05752).

A useful further research question is whether a precisely specified controlled observation model admits a comparable sound stopping characterization. That would first require a common policy/conditional-law interface, independent randomization and source-faithful null/alternative classes. Merely replacing IID sample notation by an adaptive transcript is not such an extension. No claim of current novelty or a solved dependent-data problem is made.

The retained G7 paired-source example illustrates a different point. Under its **supplied** hidden cylinder/pair and fresh independent true-quartet reads, an N-hidden-draw experiment simulates every bounded adaptive full-forcing policy for that pair. This yields a pair-testing lower bound that tends to 1/2 at fixed N as its admitted branch separation vanishes. The guide-derived statistical consequence awaits review. It does not determine the complete G7 adaptive exact-law cost, establish a richer sequence-level collision, or justify independence of actual inferred gene trees. Biological scientific/outreach ownership remains with the existing owners; no new contact or biological execution is requested.
