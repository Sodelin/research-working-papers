# Sequence and pattern reading editions

These two complete reading editions preserve written mathematical exposition by dot (OpenAI), prepared on 4 October 2026. Sodelin directed and organized the research. Their mathematical bodies are reproduced byte-for-byte from a later preserved library copy; see the [source notes](../../PROVENANCE.md). The editions are working research, with no claim of independent human peer review or established priority.

1. [Thue–Morse first occurrences, matching heights and juggling](Thue-Morse-Hand-Proofs.md) collects hand proofs of three stated first-occurrence formulas and further consequences. A separate [seven-source Lean package](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/tree/3bb3cdc9992dd14369bc5b1299ca18fdfd9e93ed/research/2026-10-04-dot-thue-morse-first-occurrence-lean-0550z/package) and historical build/audit receipts cover the named first-occurrence statements. The juggling, natural-boundary, average-height and cutoff consequences are separate hand results, not claimed as Lean-verified by that package. In the first formula the plus-family exponent starts at 2.
2. [Pattern hierarchies and selected-digit words](Pattern-Hierarchies-and-Selected-Digit-Proofs.md) collects proofs and comparisons for grouped morphisms and a distinct selected-binary-digit family. It carries an explicit correction: Cloitre's revised 2026 paper already supplied an effective all-level recurrence and seed. Historical novelty and exact comparison with prior work remain open; there is no Lean claim for this edition.

The editions report independent hand acceptance by another AI reviewer for their stated arguments. That is internal AI review, not external specialist review. The archival source retained the complete reading bodies but did not recover every original separately hashed component, checker or review receipt. A copy of the text does not recreate missing receipts or independently verify any theorem.

The separate [period-doubling proof and checker package](https://github.com/Sodelin/Research-Commons/tree/64e1fa9f532439e5f63b660d295dc6b33dfe7ec0/research/2026-10-04-dot-period-doubling-nonregularity-1150z) is already public. It reports AI-assisted review and computer checks, with no Lean claim; its translation to the original question remains for specialist review.
