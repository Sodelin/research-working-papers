## A concrete validity condition

`JCFiniteTree` accepts arbitrary `Fin 6` leaf labels, including duplicates and missing labels, and arbitrary real edge attenuations. That generality is useful for algebra but cannot silently serve as a six-sample genealogy. For example, let both children of a branch be `.tip 0`, give both terminal weights `nucleotideCharacter`, and set both attenuations to `1/2`. The candidate `jc_pair_spines_character` expression gives `1/4`: it algebraically treats the two tip occurrences as separate draws. If both occurrences were instead identified with the *same* sample variable `X₀`, its character product would be `chi(X₀)^2=1`. Therefore the recursion cannot be identified with a six-label locus law for an arbitrary tree. This is a mathematical counterexample to an unrestricted adapter, not a claim that the Lean candidate itself asserts such an adapter.

Similarly, a naive site mass formed by averaging indicator-weighted `jcTreeLikelihood` over a uniform root, but allowing the one-tip tree `.tip 0`, assigns `1/4` to each of the `4^6` six-label patterns. Its total would be `4^5`, not one. A source adapter must at least require that each of the six labels occurs exactly once, and show the relevant time-derived attenuations define nonnegative stochastic transitions. Actual pair-MRCA decomposition and equal contemporary root-to-tip elapsed times require their own structural and timing facts.

### A proposed deterministic compatibility argument
