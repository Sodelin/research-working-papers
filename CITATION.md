# Citing this working collection

This versioned working collection can be cited as:

> Sodelin, organizer. *Working Research Papers: A Curated AI-Assisted Research Collection*, version 0.17.0, 3 November 2026. [GitHub release](https://github.com/Sodelin/research-working-papers/releases/tag/v0.17.0).

Sodelin's role above is organizing and sharing this collection. It is not a
claim of scientific authorship of every underlying argument. The individual
editions identify their sources and credit the AI-authored proofs and reviews;
the final human scientific author lists have not been established. The
versioned release is not a DOI or journal publication.

For a particular argument, cite its title and the pinned edition as well as the
collection, and follow the source-specific attribution and status in the text:

- [Two-site identification in a fixed three-taxon pulse model](https://github.com/Sodelin/research-working-papers/blob/v0.17.0/manuscripts/msci/MANUSCRIPT.md)
- [Distinguishing an impossible event from a rare event](https://github.com/Sodelin/research-working-papers/blob/v0.17.0/manuscripts/calibration/MANUSCRIPT.md)
- [Two observations do not make hidden probabilities deterministic](https://github.com/Sodelin/research-working-papers/blob/v0.17.0/manuscripts/product-coin/MANUSCRIPT.md)
- [Two coordinates for exchangeable three-lineage forests](https://github.com/Sodelin/research-working-papers/blob/v0.17.0/manuscripts/cap-three/MANUSCRIPT.md)
- [How a fixed model's observable law changes with its parameters](https://github.com/Sodelin/research-working-papers/blob/v0.17.0/manuscripts/g6-fixed-cell/MANUSCRIPT.md)
- [Hand proofs: Thue–Morse first occurrences, matching heights, and juggling](https://github.com/Sodelin/research-working-papers/blob/v0.17.0/manuscripts/sequence/Thue-Morse-Hand-Proofs.md)
- [Pattern hierarchies and selected-digit words](https://github.com/Sodelin/research-working-papers/blob/v0.17.0/manuscripts/sequence/Pattern-Hierarchies-and-Selected-Digit-Proofs.md)

These are working materials. The source texts distinguish reported formal
checks from unchecked bridges, and AI reviews from independent human peer
review. See the [source and release notes](PROVENANCE.md) for the exact
selection and later changes.
