# Citing this working collection

The preserved collection can be cited as:

> Sodelin, organizer. *Working Research Papers: A Curated AI-Assisted Research Collection*, version 0.16.2, 3 November 2026. Software Heritage: [swh:1:dir:53126b1dc8e1e3ffeb6f6268ae42bf78a5b61a5a](https://archive.softwareheritage.org/swh:1:dir:53126b1dc8e1e3ffeb6f6268ae42bf78a5b61a5a/). [GitHub release](https://github.com/Sodelin/research-working-papers/releases/tag/v0.16.2).

Sodelin's role above is organizing and sharing this collection. It is not a
claim of scientific authorship of every underlying argument. The individual
editions identify their sources and credit the AI-authored proofs and reviews;
the final human scientific author lists have not been established. The archive
identifier refers to a source snapshot, not a DOI or journal publication.

For a particular argument, cite its title and the pinned edition as well as the
collection, and follow the source-specific attribution and status in the text:
