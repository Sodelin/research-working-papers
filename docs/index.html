Comments and collaboration

Sodelin welcomes expert comments, corrections, relevant prior work and properly credited collaboration, including conversations about funded research or paid project roles. Open an issue to start a discussion, or consult the individual papers for their source-specific questions.

These are working research materials, not journal publications or claims of independent human peer review. AI contributions and any reported formal checks are described in the papers; the checks apply only to their stated scope. Open questions and missing sources remain identified in the guides.
