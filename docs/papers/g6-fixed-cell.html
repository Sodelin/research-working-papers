How a fixed model's observable law changes with its parameters

Working research material. Sodelin organizes and shares this AI-assisted project. Contributions, scientific authorship and rights for eventual publications remain open. Source-specific AI attribution and verification limits are in the manuscript; the work has not received independent human specialist review.

Abstract

For a finite observed alphabet, suppose the probability of each outcome is given by one fixed polynomial in coordinates lying in the unit cube. A coefficient-and-degree sum bounds how much the observable probability law can change when the coordinates change. We explain the elementary argument and identify an existing formal component that applies it to a specified ancestral model on one fixed graph and date/cut cell. The source-model construction needed for that application is a separate part of the result.
