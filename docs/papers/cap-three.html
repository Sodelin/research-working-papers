Two coordinates for exchangeable three-lineage forests

Working research material. Sodelin organizes and shares this AI-assisted project. Contributions, scientific authorship and rights for eventual publications remain open. Source-specific AI attribution and verification limits are in the manuscript; the work has not received independent human specialist review.

Abstract

For a label-exchangeable, projective law on rooted binary forests of at most three fresh lineages, two separation probabilities determine the entire unranked forest law. Under an explicit serial-kernel assumption, the two coordinates multiply, so reversing two stages gives the same law through three lineages. We give a four-label matching example showing that the two coordinates do not determine a larger forest law. These are statements about specified abstract forest laws; physical realization and timed or observed data require additional arguments.
