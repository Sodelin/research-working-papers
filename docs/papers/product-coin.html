Two observations do not make hidden probabilities deterministic

Working research material. Sodelin organizes and shares this AI-assisted project. Contributions, scientific authorship and rights for eventual publications remain open. Source-specific AI attribution and verification limits are in the manuscript; the work has not received independent human specialist review.

Abstract

A small number of observations may be linear functions of a full probability law while being nonlinear functions of the parameters of an independent product law. We give two simple expected observations that constrain every one of arbitrarily many Bernoulli probabilities to lie strictly between zero and one. Thus a bound on the number of informative affine observations cannot by itself be used as a bound on the number of non-deterministic factors in a fitting product law. The example does not contradict a policy theorem whose conclusion concerns queries rather than factors.
