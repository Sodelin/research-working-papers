Distinguishing an impossible event from a rare event

Working research material. Sodelin organizes and shares this AI-assisted project. Contributions, scientific authorship and rights for eventual publications remain open. Source-specific AI attribution and verification limits are in the manuscript; the work has not received independent human specialist review.

Abstract

An event that has not appeared in a finite sample may be impossible or merely rare. We give the exact optimal errors for a restricted binary testing problem: one model always produces zero, while the other produces independent ones with a known, fixed probability. The optimal sum of the two error probabilities is the chance of an all-zero sample under the alternative; the optimal worst error is that chance divided by one plus itself. Both bounds are attained, including for an empty sample. The result quantifies finite-sample uncertainty under explicit assumptions and does not decide the corresponding question for arbitrary biological histories or imperfect observation.
